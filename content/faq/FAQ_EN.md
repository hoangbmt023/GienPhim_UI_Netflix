
## 1. What is GienPhim?

GienPhim is a non-profit movie streaming and discovery website designed with a cinematic-style interface to provide the best movie browsing and watching experience. All content data on GienPhim is automatically collected from free movie-sharing APIs available on the Internet.

## 2. Do I need to pay to watch movies?

**No.** GienPhim is completely free. We do not require users to recharge, purchase subscriptions, or pay any fees to access content on the website.

## 3. Why are some movies not working?

Since GienPhim does not directly host video files and only embeds content from third-party servers, some sources may occasionally become overloaded, under maintenance, or the original files may have been removed. In such cases, you can try switching to another "Server" (if available) or report the issue to us.

## 4. Can I upload my own movies to GienPhim?

**Absolutely NOT.** GienPhim is not a community-based movie upload platform. You can only create an account to save your watch history and personal favorite movie list.

## 5. How can I watch movies on a Smart TV?

GienPhim is designed to be compatible with all screen sizes. Simply open the web browser on your Smart TV, access the GienPhim website, and enjoy watching.

## 6. Why do I need to press Play twice on iPhone?

This is a core security mechanism of iOS (Apple). Safari prevents third-party embedded videos from autoplaying unless the user directly interacts with the video frame. Therefore, you may need to tap once to remove the overlay interface and tap again to start playback.

## 7. How can I request a new movie?

You can visit the **Contact & Report** page, select the "Request a New Movie" category, and enter the movie title you would like to request. We will try our best to find a source and update it as soon as possible.
