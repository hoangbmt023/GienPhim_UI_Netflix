
## 1. Account Data Collection

To personalize the user experience, GienPhim allows users to create accounts.

* Your account is only used to **store watch history and favorite movie lists**.
* We are committed to not collecting sensitive personal information beyond the basic data required to maintain your account.

## 2. User Permissions

Users on GienPhim **do not have permission** to upload, publish, add, or manage movie data. All account features are limited to viewing and tracking content only.

## 3. Local Storage

To improve user experience (for example: saving display settings or storing watch history for easier tracking), the system may use Local Storage technology directly within your browser.

* All such data **exists only on your computer or mobile device**.
* We **do not upload, synchronize, or store** any Local Storage data on GienPhim servers.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Although GienPhim does not create tracking cookies itself, the website embeds video players from third-party providers.

* These video providers may place their own cookies in your browser to collect anonymous data (such as view counts, network quality, or their own advertising purposes).
* The use of these cookies is governed by the respective provider’s privacy policy and is outside the control of GienPhim.

## 5. Secure Connection

We are committed to maintaining secure connections through HTTPS protocol encryption, helping protect the data transferred between your browser and GienPhim servers from unauthorized access or interception.
